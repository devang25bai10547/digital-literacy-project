UPI fraud is one of the most common cybercrimes in India. It occurs when fraudsters trick users into sharing sensitive details like OTPs or approving fake payment requests.

In a typical scenario, the victim receives a call or message pretending to be from a bank or payment service. The fraudster may ask the user to click a link or approve a payment request. Once the victim does this, money is instantly transferred from their account.

Students and elderly people are often targeted because they may not be fully aware of such scams. These attacks can lead to financial loss and emotional stress.

This highlights the importance of being cautious while using digital payment platforms and not trusting unknown sources.
